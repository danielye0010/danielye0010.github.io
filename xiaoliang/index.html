About

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Iowa State University, advised by {{ p.advisor }}. I work at the intersection of nonconvex optimization, signal processing, and tensor methods.

Much of my research asks a geometric question: when does a simple first-order method, started from a generic point, provably reach the right answer for a structured low-rank recovery problem? I study the optimization landscapes of these problems—spurious minima, strict saddles, and local curvature—in settings such as simultaneous blind deconvolution and phase retrieval and low-tubal-rank tensor sensing. I also design scalable algorithms for tensor-structured statistical models.

Before Iowa State, I studied computer science at Washington University in St. Louis and data science at North China University of Technology.
