## About Me

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Industrial Engineering at <a href="https://www.imse.iastate.edu/" target="_blank">Iowa State University</a>, where I'm advised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=FNN93g0AAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank">Dr. Qing Li</a> and am a member of the <a href="https://www.cnde.iastate.edu/" target="_blank">Center for Nondestructive Evaluation</a>. I'm motivated by the goal of building computational models that can understand, reason about, and predict the physical world. My current work focuses on representing and modeling 3D deformation in additive manufacturing: how and why a printed part departs from its intended CAD geometry. I also spend time on 3D deep learning, geometry processing, and generative 3D.

I studied Statistics and Economics at the <a href="https://www.d.umn.edu/" target="_blank">University of Minnesota Duluth</a>, then did a Master's in Statistics at the <a href="https://cdis.wisc.edu/" target="_blank">University of Wisconsin–Madison</a>, where I worked on research computing infrastructure in the <a href="https://it.wisc.edu/about/division-of-information-technology/research-cyberinfrastructure-2/" target="_blank">Office of the CTO</a>. In between, I worked in journalism in my hometown on reporting and documentary projects.

Outside of research, I enjoy reading and writing. More recently, I published a short story collection, <a href="https://www.amazon.com/%E9%9B%BB%E6%A2%AF%E8%A3%A1%E6%B2%92%E6%9C%89%E6%B5%B7-Traditional-Chinese-Daniel-Ye-ebook/dp/B0H14C6DS1" target="_blank"><i>No Sea in the Elevator</i></a>, about cities, systems, and the people caught inside them.
