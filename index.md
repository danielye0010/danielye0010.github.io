## About Me

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Industrial Engineering at <a href="https://www.imse.iastate.edu/" target="_blank">Iowa State University</a>, advised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=FNN93g0AAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank">Dr. Qing Li</a> and a member of the <a href="https://www.cnde.iastate.edu/" target="_blank">Center for Nondestructive Evaluation</a>.

**I build computational models to understand the physical world.** My current research focuses on representing, modeling, and correcting 3D deformation in additive manufacturing: how and why a printed part departs from its intended CAD geometry, and how that deviation can be reduced. My broader interests include 3D deep learning, spatial intelligence, and generative 3D.

I studied Statistics and Economics at the <a href="https://www.d.umn.edu/" target="_blank">University of Minnesota Duluth</a>, then did a Master's in Statistics at the <a href="https://cdis.wisc.edu/" target="_blank">University of Wisconsin–Madison</a>, where I also worked on research computing infrastructure in the <a href="https://it.wisc.edu/about/division-of-information-technology/research-cyberinfrastructure-2/" target="_blank">Office of the CTO</a>. In between, I worked in journalism in my hometown on reporting and documentary projects.
