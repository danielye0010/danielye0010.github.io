## About Me

<!-- I'm a <a href="https://med.nyu.edu/departments-institutes/population-health/divisions-sections-centers/biostatistics/" target="_blank"> Statistics</a> Ph.D. candidate at <a href="https://www.nyu.edu/" target="_blank"> New York University</a>, -->

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Industrial Engineering at <a href="https://www.imse.iastate.edu/" target="_blank">Iowa State University</a>, where I'm advised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=FNN93g0AAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank">Dr. Qing Li</a> and am a member of the <a href="https://www.cnde.iastate.edu/" target="_blank">Center for Nondestructive Evaluation</a>. I'm motivated by building computational models that help us understand, reason about, and predict the physical world. My current research focuses on representing, modeling, and correcting 3D deformation in additive manufacturing: how and why a printed part departs from its intended CAD geometry, and how that deviation can be reduced. My broader interests include 3D deep learning, geometry processing, and generative 3D.

I studied Statistics and Economics at the <a href="https://www.d.umn.edu/" target="_blank">University of Minnesota Duluth</a>, then did a Master's in Statistics at the <a href="https://cdis.wisc.edu/" target="_blank">University of Wisconsin–Madison</a>, where I also worked on research computing infrastructure in the <a href="https://it.wisc.edu/about/division-of-information-technology/research-cyberinfrastructure-2/" target="_blank">Office of the CTO</a>. In between, I worked in journalism in my hometown on reporting and documentary projects.

Outside of research, I enjoy reading and writing. More recently, I published a short story collection, <a href="https://www.amazon.com/%E9%9B%BB%E6%A2%AF%E8%A3%A1%E6%B2%92%E6%9C%89%E6%B5%B7-Traditional-Chinese-Daniel-Ye-ebook/dp/B0H14C6DS1" target="_blank"><i>No Sea in the Elevator</i></a>, about cities, systems, and the people caught inside them.
